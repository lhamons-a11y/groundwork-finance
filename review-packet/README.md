# Groundwork review packet

For a simulated user review in a Claude chat. Drag this whole folder into a chat or a Project, then paste
`REVIEW_PROMPT.md` as the first message.

- `OVERVIEW.md` - what Groundwork is, who it is for, and what it promises. The reviewer reads this first.
- `REVIEW_PROMPT.md` - the instructions and the user groups to simulate.
- `screens/` - every page of the app, full length, with the sample household loaded.

## About the screenshots

- Taken 2026-10-21 from master at `52275e6` (the research cards with the household's own figures, and the sample
  household's working-picture record), at 1280 wide. A long page is one tall image.
- The household is **the Morgans**, Groundwork's built-in sample: made up, six months of spending up to Oct 21, 2026,
  two paychecks, a credit card, a car loan, a student loan, two savings goals, three funds, a medical bill. Every
  business name is invented. The black bar at the bottom of each page ("Sample household (made up)") is part of the app
  while the sample is loaded.
- The local AI is **off** in these screenshots, so pages show Groundwork's own wording and no AI-written sentences.
  With AI on, some pages add a short narrated paragraph; nothing else changes.
- Working picture has four tabs, so it appears four times (04-07).
