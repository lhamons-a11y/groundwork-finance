Groundwork 1.0 is released.

## What's new in 1.0

**Debt reconciliation** — for the ~40% of users who track a card manually instead of importing statements:
- Enter charges and payments directly on a debt account, with semantic Charged/Payment/Interest labels and a running total
- Link a checking-account payment to a debt entry — from either side, no double-entry, and unlinking keeps both records intact
- Save a payment pattern as recurring: a draft appears a few days before it's due, confirm as-is, edit the amount, or skip that month
- Your debt ledger now actually moves the account balance, so the avalanche/snowball payoff projection reflects what you've really paid — not just what you told it once

**Adaptive learning** — six features that learn from your own financial history, not a generic model:
- Category suggestions when entering a charge, from your own merchant history
- Budget variance learning — see which categories run stable vs. volatile, with a suggested buffer
- Trip spending forecast — past trips to a region adjust a new trip's estimate
- Recurring-transaction detection — spots subscription-shaped patterns in your ledger and offers to add them to your budget
- Payoff confidence scoring — Distance Traveled shows a real confidence score from your payment reliability, not just a date
- AI gate health — see what the validation gate has caught before it ever reached your screen

**Historian** — underneath the two features above, a structured record of what happened vs. what was expected, so the system keeps getting more accurate as you use it.

## What's already there

- Complete ledger: CSV/OFX/PDF import, manual entry, reconciliation
- Budget tracking with category alerts
- Debt payoff planning (avalanche/snowball)
- Net worth tracking
- Trip planning with real regional forecasting
- Auto-import watcher (Downloads folder)
- Export encryption

## What's different about Groundwork

- **Local AI, by default.** Nothing leaves your machine — narratives are generated by a local model (phi3.5 via Ollama), not a cloud API.
- **A validation gate, not a promise.** Every number a narrative states has to trace back to your actual data. Fabricated figures are caught and rejected before you ever see them — this isn't a claim, it's mechanically enforced (`R8_number_provenance`), and you can see what it's caught in Settings.
- **Honest, not motivational.** Below-plan months and under-budget categories get reported the same neutral way as wins. No cheerleading, no shame.

## Requirements

- Windows 11, macOS (Apple Silicon), or Ubuntu 24.04 LTS
- 8GB RAM minimum (16GB recommended for local AI)
- Ollama (auto-installs via setup)
