# Item 104 — When money feels like too much

**RULED 2026-10-19 by Rob:** *"Now we are dealing with finances so people may start feeling depressed, angry, even talk
about hurting themselves. What I would like to add when that is the case is a calming voice that tells them it will be
ok and that finances are only temporary but fixable and let them know their are crisis help lines out there and link
them to it. We should do extensive research on this and make sure to add that."*

## What the research said (2026-10-19)

- **Money and suicide risk are linked.** A 2026 US systematic review found most studies tie debt to suicidality; a
  JAMA Network Open study of veterans found falling behind on a mortgage raised suicide risk by about 40% and a
  repossession nearly doubled it, with three or more money stressors more than doubling the odds. A sudden money
  shock raises risk quickly. A personal finance app is where people look at their hardest numbers.
- **Safe messaging** (Action Alliance's Framework for Successful Messaging, suicidepreventionmessaging.org; NIMH's
  five action steps): offer hope, help-seeking and resources; never present it as inevitable or unsolvable; don't
  minimise; help the person connect to people (988, trusted others). Never describe methods.
- **Chatbots** (Psychiatric Times; VERA-MH; the 2025 study of mental-health agents): say plainly it is a machine,
  point consistently to human help and 988, match the response to urgency, and don't leave it to a model - studies
  found a fifth of agents answered inconsistently or inappropriately.
- **The lines**, each checked on its own site: 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline (call or text 988, chat at
  988lifeline.org, Spanish text and chat, Deaf / hard of hearing page; free, private, 24/7); Crisis Text Line (text
  HOME to 741741); Veterans Crisis Line (988 then press 1, text 838255, chat); The Trevor Project (1-866-488-7386,
  text START to 678-678); findahelpline.com (vetted lines in 175+ countries, by ThroughLine); 911 for immediate danger.
  For the money: NFCC nonprofit credit counseling (1-800-388-2227, nfcc.org) and 211 (help with rent, utilities and
  bills, by United Way).

## What it does (K1-K5)

**K1. Despair or self-harm comes first.** Words of suicide, self-harm, wanting to die, being "better off without me",
"worth more dead", a life policy as the way out, or harming someone else bring the support message - before any
command, page, guide answer, AI, or the unanswered list. The same check runs in the ? guide's search, which is there
with AI off. It opens: *"You matter more than any bill."*, says it is glad they said it and that they needn't carry
it alone, then Rob's words - it will be okay; money trouble is temporary and can be fixed; how you feel can change
too - with 988 (Call, Text, Chat, En español) first and largest, then *"If you or someone else is in danger right
now, call 911"*, then the other lines, then the money help.

**K2. Stress and anger get the calmer message.** Hopeless, overwhelmed, drowning, panic, "I can't do this", "this
debt is killing me", angry: *"Take a breath. You're not alone in this."* - the feeling makes sense; it will be okay;
money trouble is temporary and fixable, one step at a time; NFCC and 211 first; 988 if it is ever more than money;
and a softer close - "There's no rush. Whenever you feel up to it, Groundwork can help you map out a way through, one small step at a time." with "Take a gentle look" (Debt Payoff; Rob 2026-10-19 asked it softer).

**K3. Everyday money talk is left alone.** "Kill a subscription", "give up coffee", "hurt my credit score", "when I
die" about a debt, "life insurance" on its own: none of these trigger it (s247 holds a list both ways).

**K4. Fixed words, kept nowhere.** Written once, never generated: the AI never sees what was typed, nothing is saved,
logged or sent, and the typed words are cleared and not shown back. It says Groundwork is a program, not a person,
and can't call anyone. The guide also has an entry anyone can find: *"Money worries feel like too much. Who can I
talk to?"*

**K5. Only safe links leave.** Each button opens a web page in the person's browser, or a `tel:` / `sms:` link with
digits only (a Mac hands it to FaceTime or Messages, placing it through an iPhone nearby).

## To keep it right

The numbers change rarely but do change (988's LGBTQ+ youth option ended in July 2025 and has since been restored).
Check each line on its own site before every release; s247 holds the current ones.
