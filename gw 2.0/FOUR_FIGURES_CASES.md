# U6 — four figures for one balance: one proposition, or four?

> Status: **Case set, 2026-09-12. Evidence mode. No decision, no representation, no rank, no
> resolver.** Fifth fence opened in evidence mode. K8 reached it and stopped; R-ASSERT left it
> the most isolated proposition-space question on the board.
>
> **The question, as the record already poses it** (`VALUE_DISAGREEMENT_CASES.md`, U6):
>
> > *Four derived facts, each rank 3, no relation between them, and no way to say whether they
> > are **four observations of one proposition** (the current balance) or **four different
> > propositions** (billed · believed · stated on a date · computed from payments) that
> > superficially share a name.*
>
> It could not be answered then because there was no vocabulary to answer it in. There is now:
> **P-subject** (what a document states), **P-world** (what is true — §8.2a's premise),
> **P-ack** (the entitled party's stated position, dated), held vs asserted referents, and
> the ruled sequence *attached → acknowledged → discharged*. The case runs the four figures
> through that vocabulary and nothing else.
>
> **The original figures** (`USER_KNOWLEDGE_CASES.md` U6):
>
> ```text
> the bill says            $4,000
> the user says            $3,500
> a statement says         $3,700
> payment history implies  $3,176
> ```
>
> **Standing principle this case must not erode:** *information quantity and evidentiary
> certainty are independent axes; more documents can permit fewer conclusions.* And U6's own
> honest report — *four figures, their sources, their dates, and no fifth number manufactured
> to reconcile them* — is the sentence the case has to be able to build.
>
> **Fence.** Strength of any figure; how a same-proposition conflict is resolved beyond K7's
> date-then-channel; representation; `remaining`'s projection; U6's *ladder* (the rungs U0–U7
> are not reopened).

## 0. Each figure, asked what it is

Before any pair is compared, each figure is asked the only question the vocabulary has:
**what proposition is this, about what subject, from whom, as of when?**

| figure | what it is | proposition | subject | as-of |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **the bill says $4,000** | a document P issued at the start; held or asserted referent | **P-ack** — P's stated position: *amount owed at issue* | P's stance | bill date *d₀* |
| **a statement says $3,700** | a document P issued later; held or asserted referent | **P-ack** — P's stated position: *balance* | P's stance | statement date *d₂* |
| **payment history implies $3,176** | **not evidence** — Groundwork's own derivation: gross − payments the household *attached* | a **projection** over the household's record; rests on attachment, which the ruling says is *unacknowledged* until P says otherwise | Groundwork's computation | now |
| **the user says $3,500** | testimony — but *about what?* Two readings, and the figure as written does not say which | either **R-ASSERT** (*"the last statement I saw said $3,500"* — a described document, if it individuates) or **P-world belief** (*"I think I owe about $3,500"* — a claim about the world by a party who is not knowing for P-ack and cannot be knowing for P-world) | a document, or the world | unstated |

**Forces finding 1, before any comparison.** The four figures are **not four instances of one
kind.** Two are P-ack at two dates; one is a projection; one is testimony whose proposition
depends on whether it names a document. **They share a word — *balance* — not a proposition.**

---

## Case F1 — the bill against the statement: $4,000 vs $3,700

Two P-ack claims, both from P, at *d₀* and *d₂*.

- **Same as-of?** No. The acknowledgment ruling's rule applies first: *date first, then
  channel.* Two dated positions from the same party are **two facts, not a disagreement** —
  and the $300 between them is **history**: payments P acknowledged between *d₀* and *d₂*, or
  an adjustment P applied. The record can say *"P billed $4,000; by d₂ P's statement showed
  $3,700"* and both are true.
- **Force the dates equal.** A bill and a statement both dated *d₀*, $4,000 and $3,700. Now
  one proposition — P's position at *d₀* — with two claims from one issuer. Two artefacts, one
  source: D4's *source conflict* shape, and since the source is the same party, it is **P
  disagreeing with itself across two documents** — a self-reversal by P, which is exactly what
  `resolution()`'s source-history kinds describe. (Two held artefacts from P are not a parse
  dispute — nothing was re-read; two things were read.)

**Forces finding 2.** Two P-ack figures at different dates are **never a disagreement**; the
difference is what happened between them. At one date they are a same-proposition conflict of
the source-history kind. **"More documents permit fewer conclusions" holds only at one as-of**;
across dates, more documents permit *more* — they are the history.

## Case F2 — the statement against the computation: $3,700 vs $3,176

P's stated position at *d₂* against Groundwork's *gross − attached payments*.

- **Not a disagreement between claims.** One side is P's claim; the other is a projection over
  the household's own record. They are not competing to be the same proposition.
- **The $50 is diagnostic, not disputed.** Computed *below* stated means the household attached
  more payment than P has acknowledged: one $50 payment is *attached, unacknowledged* (S2's
  shape — misdirected, in transit, not posted) — **or** P applied a $50 charge the record does
  not hold (gap 6's `record_adjustment`, unrecorded). The discrepancy **is information about
  acknowledgment state**, which is exactly what K8 found and stopped at.
- *Sentence:* *"P's statement shows $3,700. Your attached payments total $350, which would
  leave $3,176. Either a $50 payment has not been acknowledged, or P applied a $50 charge we
  don't hold."* True, and it names both branches rather than choosing.

**Forces finding 3.** The computed figure is **not evidence and does not belong in the set of
claims.** Its role is to be compared *against* P-ack to reveal acknowledgment state. Treating
it as a fifth claim about the balance is the category error that made U6 look like four
claims.

## Case F3 — the user's $3,500, both readings

**F3a — as a document report.** *"The last statement I saw said $3,500."* R-ASSERT: if it
individuates (which statement? P's, dated when?), it is P-subject with an asserted referent —
POSE and FIND, no RE-READ — and the record can ask whether it is the same statement as the
$3,700 one (a testimony conflict with a held reading, resolvable by re-reading the held
artefact, since one side *is* held) or a different one (F1's shape). If it does not
individuate — *"a statement I saw once"* — RA6: **no referent**, and it collapses to F3b.

**F3b — as a belief.** *"I think I owe $3,500."* The user is not a knowing party for P-ack
(K5) and nobody is knowing for P-world (§8.2a). This is **the weakest thing in the set and
it is not about the same proposition as any other figure.** It is the household's expectation
— which is real, and which the sentence must not omit (absence is not zero) — but it competes
with nothing: *"you believe $3,500; we hold no document for that figure"* is the whole of what
can be said.

**Forces finding 4.** The user's figure **has no place in the set until its proposition is
known**, and the figure as U6 wrote it does not say. The interface principle already ruled
applies: ask which — *did a document say that, or is it what you believe?* — rather than
assign it a kind by default.

## Case F4 — a same-proposition conflict, isolated

To find the *one* configuration among the four that is genuinely "one proposition, competing
claims": P's statement dated *d₂* says $3,700; a captured portal view (held, C2) dated *d₂*
shows $3,176.

- Same proposition: P-ack-balance at *d₂*. Same date. Two channels.
- **K7 applies:** E's issued statement outranks the servicer's live view. Not by count, not by
  position; by channel. Both held, so the conflict is examinable — either can be re-read.
- This is what U6 imagined all four figures were, and **it takes two P-ack claims at one date
  from two channels to produce it.** None of U6's original four pairs is this.

**Forces finding 5.** A genuine same-proposition disagreement about a balance requires **two
claims of the P-ack kind at one as-of**. It resolves by channel (K7). It is the only
configuration in which *more evidence, fewer conclusions* bites — and it was not among U6's
four.

## Case F5 — the fifth number

The temptation the original case named: reconcile. Latest wins (`[-1]`, fourth instance);
average; the statement because it is "official"; the computation because it is "ours".

- Each manufactures a figure no source stated — V6's *absence forced through a channel
  acquires that channel's meaning*, now with four presences instead of one absence.
- **The honest report is now buildable, and it is not a number:**

> *"P billed $4,000 on d₀. P's statement on d₂ shows $3,700. Your attached payments total $350,
> so you would expect $3,176 — either a $50 payment is not yet acknowledged or P applied a $50
> charge we don't hold. You believe $3,500; we hold no document for that."*

Every sentence true; every figure with its kind, source and date; nothing reconciled.

**Forces finding 6.** U6's *honest report* is expressible in the current vocabulary with **no
new proposition kind.** What the original model lacked was not a way to reconcile four figures
but a way to say what each one *was*.

## What the cases force

1. **The four figures share a word, not a proposition** (§0). Two are P-ack at two dates; one
   is a projection; one is testimony of undetermined kind.
2. **Two P-ack figures at different dates are history, never a disagreement** (F1). At one
   date they are a source-history conflict, correctly described by the existing resolution
   kinds.
3. **The computed figure is not evidence** (F2). Its discrepancy from P-ack is diagnostic of
   acknowledgment state — K8's finding, now general.
4. **The user's figure has no kind until asked** (F3). Document report or belief; the record
   must not assign one by default.
5. **A genuine same-proposition conflict needs two P-ack claims at one as-of** (F4), and
   resolves by channel. Not among the original four.
6. **The honest report needs no new kind** (F5) — only that each figure carries what it is.

## The fence's question, answered by the cases — not ruled

> *Four observations of one proposition, or four propositions sharing a name?*

**Neither.** The dichotomy was wrong because it assumed the four were the same *kind* of
thing. They are three kinds:

```text
E's stated position (P-ack), dated     the bill · the statement      two instances, two dates
Groundwork's projection                payment history implies       not a claim; a diagnostic
the household's testimony              the user says                 a document report, or a belief
```

Within the first kind, difference across dates is history and difference at one date is a
source-history conflict. Between the first and second, difference is acknowledgment state.
The third competes with nothing until it names a document.

**The standing principle survives, sharpened:** *more documents can permit fewer conclusions*
— **at one as-of.** Across dates, more documents are more history. Quantity and certainty stay
independent; the case says *when* they pull apart.

## Ruling — Rob, 2026-09-12. U6 lifted on exactly these terms.

*The strongest result is the rejection of the original dichotomy. There is no "four figures =
one proposition" problem to solve. The first job is kind identification.*

**Frozen distinction:**

```text
P-ack         the entitled party's stated position, always dated
projection    a Groundwork-derived result; not evidence
testimony     a household report; kind remains unresolved until identified
```

**The relationships that follow:**
- P-ack at different as-of dates → history, not disagreement.
- P-ack at the same as-of → potentially conflicting source claims.
- P-ack vs Groundwork projection → acknowledgment-state diagnostic, not claim-vs-claim.
- User figure → **no automatic classification.** Ask what the user means before giving it a
  proposition role. *The system must not silently convert "I think the balance is $3,500" into
  either a document assertion or a P-world proposition because that makes the data model
  easier.*

**The computation boundary:** the computed `$3,176` is not a fifth version of the balance. It
answers a different question — *given the payment history Groundwork currently holds, what
follows from that history?* Its disagreement with P's `$3,700` is a diagnostic finding: it can
**expose** an unacknowledged payment or an unexplained charge; it cannot **manufacture** either.
*A protection against the financial engine becoming an evidence source.*

**The standing principle, refined and frozen:**

> **More documents can permit fewer conclusions when they introduce competing claims at the
> same as-of. Across different as-of dates, additional documents may instead add history.**

**Closed as a proposition-space ambiguity without creating a new proposition kind.** Remaining
questions stay where placed and are not touched as a consequence: the exact same-date resolver
beyond date → channel; user interaction for unidentified testimony; representation of figure
kind; `remaining` and payment implementation.

**U6 lifted. No new proposition kind. No implementation decision. Evidence mode remains.**
