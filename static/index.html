Re-categorize uncategorized transactions
Find transactions still in Other or Personal and apply Groundwork's current categorization rules to them. Only those two catch-all categories are affected — anything you've already categorized manually is left alone.
Fix miscategorized paycheck deposits
A bug meant direct-deposit paychecks could be filed under Transfer instead of Income. This has been fixed for new imports — run this once to move any already-imported paychecks still sitting in Transfer over to Income.
Danger Zone
Delete every transaction in the Ledger. A backup is created automatically before anything is deleted, but this cannot be undone from within the app — use it only if you're about to re-import your full history from scratch.
Export your data
Your data belongs to you. Export a full copy at any time — transactions, budgets, net worth history, debt accounts, and settings.
